## Money and quantity representations

- No float/double columns found. Monetary model columns use numeric(18,2) or cost numeric(18,6); physical catalog numeric fields observed as numeric(18,2), numeric(18,6), and numeric(18,9).
- Quantity and unit conversion snapshots use numeric fixed-point fields; common quantity scale is 6 and conversion factor scale is 9.
- Timestamp mappings use PostgreSQL `timestamp with time zone`; business date columns use date.
- IDs are UUID with `ValueGeneratedNever` in the model (application generated UUIDv7); no PostgreSQL serial/identity sequence is used for entity IDs. Document sequence is a row-backed counter.

## Identity and cost structures

- `inventory.units`: TrackingCode and SupplierProduct/ItemSequence snapshots are nullable in the physical schema to accommodate existing lifecycle/historical states; source checks origin consistency and positive sequence when present. Persisted null identity is therefore possible through direct SQL and is reported in the findings register.
- `inventory.unit_identity_claims`: unique `(identifier_type, normalized_value)` and `(inventory_unit_id, identifier_slot)` indexes plus type/slot, normalization-version, and non-empty checks.
- `inventory.stock_balances`: unique per product and nonnegative bucket check.
- `inventory.cost_states`: unique per product and nonnegative cost/quantity check.
- `inventory.lots` and `inventory.lot_bucket_balances`: source references, bucket uniqueness and nonnegative quantity checks.
- `system.document_sequences`: row-backed sequence authority; update-on-conflict increment can roll back with transaction, unlike PostgreSQL `nextval` which is not transactional. This explicit sequence reuse characteristic needs review against the canonical no-reuse business number policy if a failed allocation is exposed externally. Current application completes document persistence in the same transaction and unique document constraints prevent collisions; a rollback/retry may reuse the number. Route to current schema/business owner for policy decision; no proof of external disclosure was found.
